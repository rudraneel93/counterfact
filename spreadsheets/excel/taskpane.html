Counterfact

=CF.EFFECT(B2:B60, 46, C2:E60) gives the verdict, effect per period, likely range and effect in %.

=CF.WITHOUT(B2:B60, 46, C2:E60) gives what the metric would have been without the change.

46 is the row (counted from the top of your range) of the first period after the change. Comparison columns are optional but make the answer sharper.
